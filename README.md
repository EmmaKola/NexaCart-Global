      <h2 align="center">Client Background</h2>
      <p align="center">
        <strong>NexaCart Global</strong> is a multinational e-commerce company offering a diverse range of consumer products through its digital marketplace. Established in 2019, the company serves customers across <strong>20 countries</strong> and operates across <strong>14 product categories</strong>.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        The NexaCart database contains approximately <strong>25,000 customer orders</strong> and transaction activity covering <strong>2020 to Q1 2026</strong>, with total revenue of approximately <strong>$2.59 million</strong>. The available data is organised into four tables: <strong>orders, customers, product summary and monthly revenue</strong>. Together, these tables provide the measures and dimensions required to evaluate sales, customer behaviour, product performance, acquisition channels, customer tiers and geographic demand.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        To support its next phase of growth, NexaCart Global commissioned a comprehensive customer and sales performance analysis covering its years of operation from <strong>2020 to Q1 2026</strong>. Reporting to the <strong>Chief Commercial Officer</strong>, the analysis is designed to provide evidence-based insights that support better commercial decisions and improve operational performance.
      </p>

<h2 align="center">Revenue Performance Story</h2>

<table align="center">
  <tr>
    <td width="480" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">2020 | A Stable Foundation</h3>
      <p align="center">
        NexaCart generated <strong>$417.18K</strong> in 2020. Quarterly revenue remained close to the benchmark: <strong>Q1 was 1.0% below average</strong>, <strong>Q2 was 1.7% above</strong>, <strong>Q3 was 1.5% below</strong> and <strong>Q4 finished 4.3% above average</strong>.
      </p>
      <p align="center">The above-average Q4 result provided the first evidence of positive year-end momentum.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="480" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">2021–2022 | Weakness and an Uneven Recovery</h3>
      <p align="center">
        Revenue declined <strong>4.3% year on year to $399.25K in 2021</strong>. Q1, Q2 and Q3 were respectively <strong>4.2%, 6.2% and 5.1% below average</strong>, before Q4 recovered to <strong>1.6% above average</strong>.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        Revenue increased <strong>2.7% to $409.93K in 2022</strong>, but performance remained volatile. <strong>Q1 fell to $91.96K, 11.1% below average and the weakest quarter in the period</strong>. The business rebounded to <strong>3.6% above average in Q2</strong>, slipped <strong>3.1% below in Q3</strong> and accelerated to <strong>7.0% above average in Q4</strong>.
      </p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="480" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">2023 | Growth Returned</h3>
      <p align="center">
        Annual revenue rose another <strong>2.8% to $421.59K</strong>. <strong>Q1 and Q2 were 2.1% and 7.0% above average</strong>, creating a strong first half.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        Revenue fell <strong>3.7% below average in Q3</strong>, then recovered to <strong>2.3% above average in Q4</strong>, demonstrating resilience despite the temporary slowdown.
      </p>
    </td>
    <td width="480" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">2024 | A Broad-Based Downturn</h3>
      <p align="center">
        Revenue declined <strong>6.2% to $395.66K</strong>, with every quarter below the benchmark: <strong>Q1 by 0.8%, Q2 by 8.6%, Q3 by 2.4% and Q4 by 5.6%</strong>.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        Because weakness extended across all four quarters, 2024 represents a sustained commercial slowdown rather than an isolated quarterly decline.
      </p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td colspan="2" width="960" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3 align="center">2025–Q1 2026 | Sustained Recovery and Record Momentum</h3>
      <p align="center">
        NexaCart generated <strong>$425.36K in 2025</strong>, an increase of <strong>7.5% from 2024</strong> and the highest full-year revenue in the reporting period. Although Q1 remained <strong>4.0% below average</strong>, performance improved to <strong>4.8% above average in Q2</strong>, <strong>6.7% above in Q3</strong> and <strong>3.9% above in Q4</strong>.
      </p>
      <p align="center">
        <strong>Q1 2026 reached $116.15K, 12.3% above the quarterly benchmark and the highest quarter in the dataset.</strong> As the 2026 data currently covers only Q1, this result should not be compared with previous full-year totals.
      </p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
