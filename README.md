# NexaCart Global E-commerce Performance Report

## Client Background

NexaCart Global is a multinational e-commerce company offering a diverse range of consumer products through its digital marketplace. Established in 2019, the company serves customers across **20 countries** and operates across **14 product categories**.

The NexaCart database contains approximately **25,000 customer orders** and transaction activity covering **2020 to Q1 2026**, with total revenue of approximately **$2.59 million**. The available data is organised into four tables: **orders, customers, product summary, and monthly revenue**. Together, these tables provide the measures and dimensions required to evaluate sales, customer behaviour, product performance, acquisition channels, customer tiers, and geographic demand.

To support its next phase of growth, NexaCart Global commissioned a comprehensive customer and sales performance analysis covering its years of operation from **2020 to Q1 2026**. Reporting to the **Chief Commercial Officer**, the analysis is designed to provide evidence-based insights that support better commercial decisions and improve operational performance.

> **Portfolio note:** NexaCart Global is a fictional company, and this project uses a synthetic e-commerce dataset for analytical and portfolio purposes.

### North-Star Metrics

1. **Sales Performance**

   Evaluate revenue trends, order volume, units sold, and average order value across time and countries.

2. **Product Performance**

   Analyse the product portfolio to identify high-performing and underperforming products, understand their commercial impact, and compare return rates across the years of operation to support product and inventory decisions.

3. **Customer Acquisition Channel and Customer Tier**

   Evaluate acquisition channels and customer tiers to identify the most effective routes to market and provide actionable insight for the NexaCart marketing team.

4. **Country Performance**

   Examine revenue, customer satisfaction, and product demand across NexaCart's countries of operation to identify strong markets and areas requiring commercial improvement.

# Executive Summary

## Revenue Remained Volatile Across the Years and Peaked in Q1 2026

![NexaCart quarterly revenue trend from 2020 to Q1 2026](reports/figures/quarterly-revenue-trend.png)

![NexaCart quarterly revenue variance from the overall quarterly average](reports/figures/quarterly-revenue-variance.png)

The revenue trend and variance charts tell a consistent story of initial stability, recurring volatility, and a strong recent recovery. The variance percentage compares each quarter with the **overall quarterly revenue benchmark of $103.41K**; it does not represent quarter-on-quarter growth.

1. **A stable foundation in 2020**
   - NexaCart generated **$417.18K** in 2020. Quarterly revenue remained close to the benchmark: **Q1 was 1.0% below average**, **Q2 was 1.7% above**, **Q3 was 1.5% below**, and **Q4 finished 4.3% above average**.
   - The above-average Q4 result provided the first evidence of positive year-end momentum.

2. **Weakness in 2021 followed by an uneven 2022 recovery**
   - Revenue declined **4.3% year on year to $399.25K in 2021**. Q1, Q2, and Q3 were respectively **4.2%, 6.2%, and 5.1% below average**, before Q4 recovered to **1.6% above average**.
   - Revenue increased **2.7% to $409.93K in 2022**, but performance was volatile. **Q1 2022 fell to $91.96K, 11.1% below average and the weakest quarter in the period**. The business then rebounded to **3.6% above average in Q2**, slipped **3.1% below in Q3**, and accelerated to **7.0% above average in Q4**.

3. **Growth returned in 2023 but was not yet consistent**
   - Annual revenue rose another **2.8% to $421.59K**. **Q1 and Q2 were 2.1% and 7.0% above average**, creating a strong first half.
   - Revenue fell **3.7% below average in Q3**, then recovered to **2.3% above average in Q4**, demonstrating resilience despite the temporary slowdown.

4. **A broad-based downturn in 2024**
   - Revenue declined **6.2% to $395.66K**, with every quarter below the benchmark: **Q1 by 0.8%, Q2 by 8.6%, Q3 by 2.4%, and Q4 by 5.6%**.
   - Because the weakness extended across all four quarters, 2024 represents a sustained commercial slowdown rather than an isolated quarterly decline.

5. **A sustained recovery in 2025 and record momentum in Q1 2026**
   - NexaCart generated **$425.36K in 2025**, an increase of **7.5% from 2024** and the highest full-year revenue in the reporting period. Although Q1 remained **4.0% below average**, performance improved to **4.8% above average in Q2**, **6.7% above in Q3**, and **3.9% above in Q4**.
   - **Q1 2026 reached $116.15K, 12.3% above the quarterly benchmark and the highest quarter in the dataset**. As the 2026 data currently covers only Q1, this result should not be compared with previous full-year totals.

### Executive Takeaway

NexaCart moved from weakness in 2021 and early 2022, through a second downturn in 2024, into a sustained recovery beginning in Q2 2025. Three consecutive above-average quarters in 2025, followed by record revenue in Q1 2026, indicate strengthening commercial momentum. The next stage of the analysis should identify the products, countries, customer tiers, and acquisition channels responsible for this acceleration and determine whether the growth can be sustained throughout the remaining quarters of 2026.
